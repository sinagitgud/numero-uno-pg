Domain Groups
🔐 Identity & Access 1 table
users

Central auth record. Firebase handles authentication; this table stores role, preferences, and links all activity to a person.

🏢 Property Structure 3 tables
properties
rooms
beds

Physical hierarchy: a Property has Rooms, each Room has Beds. A Bed is the atomic unit that gets assigned to a Tenant.

🏠 Tenancy 1 table
tenants

The pivotal record. Links a User to a Bed and Property. Stores the monthly rate, security deposit, check-in/out, and KYC details.

💳 Billing 2 tables
invoices
payments

One Invoice per tenant per month. Payments are individual transactions against an invoice (supports partial payments, UPI, cash, Razorpay).

🔧 Operations 4 tables
expenses
capex
staff
salary_records

Day-to-day opex (groceries, utilities, marketing), capital purchases, and on-ground staff payroll.

📈 Finance 1 table
investments

Tracks investor capital (inflows) and returns (outflows) with maturity dates. Standalone — not linked to properties.

🎫 Tenant Services 4 tables
support_tickets
ticket_comments
guest_logs
leave_requests

Everything a tenant does through the app: raise issues, register guests, apply for leave.

🎯 Sales Pipeline 1 table
inquiries

Prospective tenants tracked through the funnel: NEW → CONTACTED → VISIT_SCHEDULED → CONVERTED → DROPPED. Converts to a Tenant record on booking.

⚙️ Property Operations 3 tables
food_menu_items
goal_configs
rent_agreement_templates

Weekly food menus per property, monthly sales targets with incentive tiers, and reusable rent agreement templates.
